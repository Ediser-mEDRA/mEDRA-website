## DOI URL Update

This service allows registered users to update the URLs associated with DOIs registered with mEDRA independently of other DOI metadata updates.

Users can upload a list containing the DOIs and their corresponding new URLs to be applied. This service is intended specifically for updating DOI resolution URLs while leaving the other metadata associated with the DOIs unchanged.

- [**Upload New URLs**](https://www-medra-dev.medra.org/servlet/user/view_uploadForUpdateURL)  
  Service for uploading a list of DOIs and their new URLs for update.
