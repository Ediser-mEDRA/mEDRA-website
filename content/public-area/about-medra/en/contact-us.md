# 📬 Contact Us

Whether you are **new to DOIs and mEDRA services** or an **existing mEDRA customer**, our team and partners are available to assist you.

---

## 🆕 New to mEDRA?

### 🏷️ Interested in assigning DOI names with mEDRA?

If you are interested in **registering and managing DOI names with mEDRA**, you can:

- 📝 Contact us through our [**contact form**](https://www-medra-dev.medra.org/en/req_account.htm)
- 🌍 Contact one of our **mEDRA partners in your country**

### 💬 General enquiries

For any other information or request, please contact:

📧 [**medra@medra.org**](mailto:medra@medra.org)

---

## 🔐 Already a mEDRA Customer?

### 🛠️ Technical support

For technical assistance with your mEDRA services, please contact:

📧 [**support@medra.org**](mailto:support@medra.org)

When contacting our support team, please include your:

- 🔢 **DOI Prefix**
- 👤 **USERID**

This information will help us identify your account and process your request more efficiently.

### 💼 Contracts, DOI brackets & commercial information

For requests concerning:

- 🔄 Contract renewal
- 📦 Changes to your DOI bracket
- 💰 Pricing and commercial information

please contact:

📧 [**sales@medra.org**](mailto:sales@medra.org)

Please include your **DOI Prefix** and **USERID**, or contact one of our partners in your country.

### 🔗 Crossref-enabled mEDRA DOIs

Would you like to learn more about how to **"Crossref-enable" your mEDRA DOIs**?

📧 Contact our sales team at [**sales@medra.org**](mailto:sales@medra.org), including your **DOI Prefix** and **USERID**.
