# About mEDRA

**mEDRA** is a **DOI Registration Agency (DOI RA)**, appointed by the [International DOI Foundation](http://www.doi.org) on **1 July 2003**. It provides a DOI registration service for publishers, academic institutions, research organizations and intermediaries in Italy, the EU market and internationally.

## Our Mission

mEDRA's goal is to make DOI registration as simple as possible for everyone. That's why the mEDRA system can be used by:

- small and large organizations
- commercial and open-access providers
- users with and without technical expertise

mEDRA can also support its customers individually in **Italian, English, French and German**.

## International Partnerships

mEDRA cooperates on DOI registration with international partners:

| Partner | Region |

Thanks to a collaboration with Crossref, the world's largest DOI agency, it is possible—through an additional agreement—to also deposit DOI metadata registered via mEDRA on the Crossref platform.

> For more information on the DOI and related topics, see the sections [What is the DOI](https://www-medra-dev.medra.org/en/faq.htm) and [How to register DOIs](https://www-medra-dev.medra.org/en/guide.htm).

## Technical Expertise

Thanks to a complementary team combining both content and technology expertise, mEDRA has a unique blend of know-how for managing the technological standards used in the content industry.

mEDRA therefore also acts as a **technical provider** in the field of:

- identification standards
- metadata
- e-infrastructure for the publishing industry

offering tailored support in the analysis of user and business requirements, metadata design, selection and management, as well as identification-related topics.

### Projects as Technical Provider

- **OP DOI Agency** – technical provider
- **Italian ISBN Agency** – technical provider
- **LIA** – a service that makes e-books more accessible to visually impaired readers on the Italian market
- **Gedi** – a platform that supports Italian publishers with licensing

## Partner Spotlight: MVB GmbH

[MVB GmbH](http://www.mvb-online.de) is a business enterprise of the German Publishers and Booksellers Association (Börsenverein des Deutschen Buchhandels e.V.) and, as a publishing company, offers products and services for the German book trade. For example, MVB also operates the **German ISBN Agency** and the **"Verzeichnis Lieferbarer Bücher" (VLB)**, the German books-in-print database.

Since **2006**, mEDRA and MVB have been cooperating on DOI matters: MVB manages DOI registration in the German-speaking region.
