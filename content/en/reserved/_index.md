The Registered Users Area is the dedicated environment for users and organizations that use mEDRA services to register and manage DOI (Digital Object Identifier).

Through authenticated access, users can manage their account information and access the functionalities available according to their user profile, including DOI registration, metadata management, and monitoring of identified resources.

The secure area provides structured access to mEDRA services and supports the management of the DOI lifecycle and associated metadata.
