## New to DOIs and mEDRA services?

Here you find the details on how to contact us:

- **Interested in assigning DOI names with mEDRA?**  
  Contact us using our contact form or contact one of our partners in your country.
- **General enquires**  
  For any other request contact us at [medra@medra.org](mailto:medra@medra.org).

## Already a mEDRA customer?

Here you find the details on how to contact us:

- **Do you need technical support?**  
  Contact us at [support@medra.org](mailto:support@medra.org), including your DOI Prefix and your USERID.
- **Do you need to renew your contract with us, change DOI Bracket or simply get more commercial information?**  
  Contact us at [sales@medra.org](mailto:sales@medra.org), including your DOI Prefix and your USERID, or contact one of our partners in your country.
- **Do you need more info on how to "Crossref-enable" your mEDRA DOIs?**  
  Contact us at [sales@medra.org](mailto:sales@medra.org), including your DOI Prefix and your USERID.
